Privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Nat 5 Maths Scotland is a free revision app. Most people who use it are pupils aged 15 to 18, so we keep data to a minimum and explain it plainly here.

The short version

  • You don't make an account, and we never ask for your name or email.
  • Your scores, streak and settings stay on your own device.
  • We don't use cookies or advertising trackers.
  • We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or identify you.

Your progress

Your scores, streak, notes and settings are saved in your browser's local storage, on your phone or computer. They aren't sent to us. Clearing your browser data, or using a different device, starts you from scratch.

If you install the app, a service worker saves the app's files on your device so it works offline. It doesn't save anything about you.

Visit counts

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they use. It doesn't set cookies, doesn't track you across other websites, and doesn't build a profile of you. We only see totals, such as page views and the type of device.

Other companies that see your visit

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address to send you the page.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Cloudflare runs the visit counts described above.
  • Amazon: the shop section suggests revision guides, calculators and stationery, with links to Amazon UK. We may earn a small commission if someone buys through those links, at no extra cost. Nothing is shared with Amazon unless you tap a link, and after that Amazon's own privacy notice applies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what data we hold about you and ask us to delete it. As we don't collect your name, email or progress, there's normally nothing for us to hand over or delete. To remove your progress, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
