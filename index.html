Exam tips

  • Show all working. You get method marks even if the final answer is wrong.
  • Paper 1 is non-calculator, Paper 2 is calculator. Know which one you're sitting.
  • Read the question twice. Look for "exact value", "2 decimal places", "simplify", "factorise fully".
  • Check your answer. Substitute back in — does it work?
  • Sketch the triangle and label the sides before choosing SOH CAH TOA, the sine rule or the cosine rule.
  • Never leave a blank. A sensible first step can still earn a mark.
